Common questions

Fair questions before you sign up.

I already have a Google Business Profile. Isn't that enough?

A Business Profile gets you on the map, but it wasn't built to be read by AI assistants, and you don't control what shows up next to it — including competitors' ads. Your Enoma page is yours alone, built for both Google and AI search.

I already have a website. Why do I need this too?

If your current site is already sending you calls every week, you're ahead of most local businesses — you probably don't need Enoma. If it isn't, the problem usually isn't the site itself; it's that it was never built specifically to be found. Enoma is that missing piece.

How is this different from Yelp or Angi?

Directory listings put competitors right next to you and often resell your leads. Your Enoma page is indexed as your own site — no competitor ads, no shared leads, just customers who searched and found you.

Do I have to write the page myself?

No. Tell us about your business — services, area, a few notes in your own words — and AI writes the page copy. You can edit anything afterward from your dashboard.

How do AI assistants like ChatGPT actually find my business?

The same way Google does: by reading clear, structured content about who you are and what you do. Enoma pages are built in that format from day one, so both Google's crawlers and AI assistants can find and recommend you.

Will the AI-written copy actually sound like my business?

It's built from what you tell us — your services, your area, your own notes — not a generic template. And it's never locked in: log into your dashboard anytime to edit anything.

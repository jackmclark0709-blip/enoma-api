Common questions

Fair questions before you sign up.

Will this actually get me on Google?

No one can honestly promise you a ranking. What we can do is give Google exactly what it looks for from a local business — your trade, your towns, your services and your number, on a page built the way search engines read it. That's why the first month is free: you see real results before you pay.

What does it cost?

The first month is free, with no credit card. After that it's $19.99/month to keep your page live, and that includes personalized support from our team. Month-to-month, cancel anytime.

I already have a Google Business Profile. Isn't that enough?

It's a great start, and you should keep it. But a Business Profile shows competitors right next to you, and it gives Google very little to go on. A page of your own gives Google (and AI assistants like ChatGPT) more to understand about what you do and where — and gives homeowners one place to call you from.

I already have a website. Why do I need this too?

If your current site is already sending you calls every week, you're ahead of most local businesses — you probably don't need Enoma. If it isn't, the problem usually isn't the design; it's that it was never built to be found or to get someone to call. That's the part Enoma does.

How is this different from Yelp or Angi?

Directory listings put competitors right next to you and often sell the same lead to several businesses, and you pay per lead or per click whether you win the job or not. Your Enoma page is only about you — no competitor ads, no shared leads, one flat monthly price. The customer calls you directly. See the full comparison →

Do I have to write the page myself?

No. If we build it for you, it takes a 10-minute call. If you build it yourself, you fill in your services and area and AI writes the first draft. Either way you can change anything, anytime.

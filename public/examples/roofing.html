Common questions

What are the signs I need a new roof?

Curling or missing shingles, granules collecting in the gutters, daylight in the attic, or leaks that keep coming back. Asphalt shingle roofs usually last about 20 to 25 years. If yours is getting close, have it looked at before the next big storm.

How long does a new roof take?

Most homes are done in a couple of days. We'll tell you up front.

What towns do you serve?

Can a roof leak be repaired, or does the whole roof need replacing?

Many leaks come from one spot, like the flashing around a chimney or vent, or a few damaged shingles, and can be repaired. If the roof is near the end of its life or leaks in several places, replacing it may cost less in the long run.

My roof leaks around the chimney. Do I need a new roof?

Often not. Many chimney leaks are the flashing, which we can repair.

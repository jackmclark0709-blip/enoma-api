Common questions

How do I know if my water heater needs replacing?

Common signs are rusty or discolored hot water, rumbling or popping from the tank, water around the base, or hot water that runs out faster than it used to. Most tank water heaters last around 8 to 12 years. If yours is in that range and acting up, call us and we'll tell you whether a repair makes sense or it's time to replace it.

Do you come out on weekends?

Should I use a chemical drain cleaner on a clog?

We'd hold off. Store-bought drain chemicals can damage older pipes and often clear only part of the clog. If a plunger doesn't fix it, or several drains are slow at once, the blockage is probably further down the line, and that's worth having us look at.

How often should my lawn be mowed?

During the growing season, usually once a week. A good rule is to never cut more than about a third of the grass blade at once, so fast-growing spring weeks may need more.

How often should mulch be refreshed?

Most beds look best with a fresh layer every year or two. Two to three inches is plenty. Piling it against tree trunks or plant stems can cause rot.

When should spring and fall cleanups be done?

Spring cleanups usually happen once the snow is gone and the ground firms up. Fall cleanups work best after most of the leaves have dropped, before the first snow.

Common questions

Do I need a credit card to get started?

No. Your first month is completely free with no payment information required, then $19.99/month or $199/year. Cancel anytime. You only need a credit card if you decide to keep your page live after your free month.

When does my free trial end?

Your free month starts the day your lead gen page goes live. You'll see the exact expiry date in your dashboard, and we'll remind you before it ends.

What happens if I don't subscribe?

After your free month, your page turns off and shows as inactive to visitors. It isn't deleted. Subscribe for $19.99/month at any time to bring it back live.

What does the $19.99/month include?

It keeps your page live, and it includes personalized support from our team. If you want help setting your page up, or want something changed, just ask and we'll take care of it. Service pages aren't included; they're a separate add-on.

Is there a yearly plan?

Yes. After your free month it's $199/year, about $16.58 a month, which saves $40.88 compared with 12 months at $19.99. The yearly plan also gives you a real person as your account manager: text or email Jack any change and it's on your page within two business days. You choose monthly or yearly when you subscribe.

What is a Service Spotlight page? Is it included?

An extra page for one service you want more jobs in, linked from your main Enoma page. Service pages are not included in either plan: they're a separate add-on at $9.99/month per page, on top of your Enoma page.

Is there a contract?

No. Pay monthly, or once a year on the yearly plan. Cancel from your dashboard at any time and it won't renew — no fees, no questions asked.

How does payment work?

Payments are processed securely by Stripe. Enoma never stores your card details. If you subscribe during your free month, your first charge is the day the free month ends; after that you're billed every month or every year, depending on the plan you chose.

Is this a website?

Yes — a one-page website built for one job: getting a homeowner who searches for your trade in your town to call you. Unlike a directory listing, there are no competitors next to you and no shared leads. Unlike a typical business website, everything on it is there to turn a visitor into a call or a quote request.

Will it show up on Google right away?

No one can honestly promise a ranking. Your page is added to the sitemap Google crawls the day it goes live, and it is built the way Google reads local businesses, but how fast it shows up depends on your town and your trade. That's why the first month is free (then $19.99/month) — you get to see real results before you pay.

Can I update my page?

Yes. Log into your dashboard at any time to update your services, contact info, photos, and more. Changes go live immediately.

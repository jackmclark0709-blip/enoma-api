Common questions

Do I need a credit card to get started?

No. Your first month is completely free with no payment information required. You only need a credit card if you decide to subscribe after your trial.

When does my free trial end?

Your free month starts the day your lead gen page goes live. You'll see the exact expiry date in your dashboard, and we'll remind you before it ends.

What happens if I don't subscribe?

After your free month, your page will show as inactive to visitors. It won't be deleted — subscribe at any time to bring it back live.

Is there a contract?

No. Enoma is month-to-month. Cancel from your dashboard at any time — no fees, no questions asked.

How does payment work?

Payments are processed securely by Stripe. Enoma never stores your card details. You're billed monthly on the date you subscribe.

Is this a website?

Yes — a one-page website built for one job: getting a homeowner who searches for your trade in your town to call you. Unlike a directory listing, there are no competitors next to you and no shared leads. Unlike a typical business website, everything on it is there to turn a visitor into a call or a quote request.

Will it show up on Google right away?

No one can honestly promise a ranking. Your page is added to the sitemap Google crawls the day it goes live, and it is built the way Google reads local businesses, but how fast it shows up depends on your town and your trade. That's why the first month is free — you get to see real results before you pay.

Can I update my page?

Yes. Log into your dashboard at any time to update your services, contact info, photos, and more. Changes go live immediately.

Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Your page stays live on Enoma at no cost, with no trial period and no credit card. We make money when businesses choose Pro for the extra help.

Do I need a credit card to get started?

No. You only enter card details if you upgrade to Pro.

What happens if I cancel Pro?

Your page stays live on the free plan. You keep everything in Free; the Pro extras like your own domain and the monthly report stop at the end of your billing period.

Is there a contract?

No. Pro is month-to-month. Cancel from your dashboard at any time — no fees, no questions asked.

How does payment work?

Pro payments are processed securely by Stripe. Enoma never stores your card details. You're billed monthly on the date you upgrade.

Is this a website?

Yes — a one-page website built for one job: getting a homeowner who searches for your trade in your town to call you. Unlike a directory listing, there are no competitors next to you and no shared leads. Unlike a typical business website, everything on it is there to turn a visitor into a call or a quote request.

Will it show up on Google right away?

No one can honestly promise a ranking. Your page is added to the sitemap Google crawls the day it goes live, and it is built the way Google reads local businesses, but how fast it shows up depends on your town and your trade. Since it's free, you can leave it running and watch the results come in.

Can I update my page?

Yes. Log into your dashboard at any time to update your services, contact info, photos, and more. Changes go live immediately. On Pro, you can also just tell us what to change.

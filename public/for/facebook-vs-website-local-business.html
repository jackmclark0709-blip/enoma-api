Common questions

Why doesn't my Facebook page show up on Google?

Facebook's privacy settings and page structure make it difficult for Google to index and rank business pages in local search results. Google overwhelmingly prefers standalone websites for local service queries.

Do I need to choose between Facebook and a website?

No — keep your Facebook page and add an Enoma website. Facebook handles your existing community and referrals. Your Enoma page captures the customers who search Google first.

What about Google Business Profile?

GBP is worth setting up — it's free and puts you on Google Maps. But a GBP alone doesn't rank for search queries the way a dedicated website does. The most effective setup is GBP + a website working together.

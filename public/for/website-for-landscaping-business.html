Built for how people hire a landscaper

Nobody hires a landscaper from a beautiful homepage animation. They search "lawn care near me" or "fall cleanup" plus their town, look at a couple of options, and call or ask for a quote from the one that clearly does what they need, in their area, and looks reliable. A landscaping website needs to:

  • Show up for the services people actually search: lawn mowing, spring and fall cleanups, mulching, hedge trimming, hardscaping, snow removal.
  • Say where you work so the homeowner knows you cover their street before they reach out.
  • Make it easy to ask for a quote, because most landscaping work starts with "how much for my yard?"
  • Look established: photos of your work, reviews, years in business.

What's on your page

  • Every service you offer, each called out so Google and customers can match it.
  • Your service area, town by town.
  • Seasonal services up front: cleanups in spring and fall, snow removal in winter.
  • Click-to-call and a quote form that go to you only. No shared leads.
  • Photos and reviews so homeowners can see your work.

Common questions

Questions from landscapers

Do I need a website for my lawn care business if I'm already busy?

If you're booked solid with customers you like, maybe not yet. A website pays off when you want to replace customers who leave, fill gaps between seasons, raise prices, or pick better jobs. It also makes referred customers more likely to actually call.

Will my landscaping website show up on Google?

Nobody can honestly promise a ranking. Your page is built the way Google reads local businesses, with your services and towns clearly marked up, and it's added to the sitemap Google crawls when it goes live.

Can I show photos of my work?

Yes. Before and after shots of lawns, patios and cleanups are some of the most convincing things you can put on the page.

Can it handle seasonal services like snow removal?

Yes. Seasonal services like spring cleanups, fall cleanups and snow plowing can be featured when they're in season.

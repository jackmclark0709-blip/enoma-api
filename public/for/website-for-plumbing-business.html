Most plumbing websites are built to look nice. Yours should get calls.

Plumbing customers don't browse. They have a leak, a dead water heater or a backed-up drain, and they want someone on the phone in the next five minutes. A plumbing website only has a few jobs to do in that moment:

  • Show up when someone searches "plumber near me," "water heater replacement" or "emergency plumber" plus their town.
  • Look legit fast: licensed and insured, real reviews, a real local business.
  • Make calling one tap on a phone, because that's where almost all of these searches happen.
  • Catch the people who won't call with a short quote form that goes straight to your phone or inbox.

That's what an Enoma page is: one fast, focused page per business, built around your services and the towns you serve. No 12-page agency site, no monthly SEO retainer.

What's on your page

  • Your services, each called out: drain cleaning, water heaters, sewer lines, leak detection, boilers, gas lines, remodels, whatever you actually do.
  • Your service area: the towns you cover, written the way people search for them.
  • Click-to-call everywhere: in the header, in a sticky bar on mobile, and next to every service.
  • Emergency badge: if you take after-hours calls, it says so before anything else.
  • Trust signals: license number, insurance, years in business, your Google reviews.
  • A quote form that sends leads to you only. No lead marketplace, no competitors listed next to you.

Why not just use a Google Business Profile or Facebook?

Keep both. They're free and they matter. But a Business Profile only shows a few lines about you, and Facebook pages rarely show up when a stranger searches for a plumber. A website gives Google a full description of your services and towns to match against searches, and it gives the homeowner who found you through a referral somewhere to check you out before they call. Common questions

Questions from plumbers

Why do I need a website if most of my plumbing work comes from referrals?

Because most referred customers still look you up before they call. If they search your name and find nothing, or an old Facebook page, some of them call someone else. A website also catches the people searching for a plumber who haven't heard of you yet.

Will my plumbing website show up on Google?

Nobody can honestly promise a ranking. Your page is built the way Google reads local businesses, with your trade, services, towns and phone number clearly marked up, and it's added to the sitemap Google crawls when it goes live. Linking it from your Google Business Profile helps a lot.

How do emergency calls work?

Your phone number is the most prominent thing on the page and is one tap to call on mobile. If you take emergency or after-hours work, we add an emergency badge so a homeowner with water on the floor knows to call you now.

Can I list my license number and insurance?

Yes. License number, Licensed & Insured, years in business and your reviews can all go on the page. Homeowners look for them.

Your first month is free, with no credit card. After that it's $19.99 a month to keep the page live, and that includes personal support. No contract; cancel anytime.

Do I have to build it myself?

No. You can build it yourself in about 5 minutes, or we'll set it up for you from a short call. After it's live, changes are made for you when you ask.

Can I use my own domain name?

Your page starts at enoma.io/your-business-name, which Google indexes like any other site. If you already own a domain, ask us about pointing it at your page.
